<h1>Multi-Zone Mini Split Sizing Complete Guide</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 4 min read</p>

<p>When conditioning an entire home or multi-room layout with ductless heat pumps, installing a multi-zone system lets you connect several indoor air handlers to a single outdoor condenser unit. Sizing a multi-zone system requires calculating each room's BTU demand independently before selecting outdoor compressor capacity.</p>

<p>Here is how to calculate multi-zone requirements step by step.</p>

<h2>Worked Example of a 3-Zone Layout</h2>

<p>Suppose you are installing a ductless system across three distinct spaces:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Primary Bedroom (200 sq ft):</strong> 200 x 25 = 5,000 BTU (Select 9,000 BTU indoor head)</li>
    <li><strong>Living Room (400 sq ft):</strong> 400 x 25 = 10,000 BTU (Select 12,000 BTU indoor head)</li>
    <li><strong>Home Office (150 sq ft):</strong> 150 x 25 = 3,750 BTU (Select 9,000 BTU indoor head)</li>
</ul>

<h2>Selecting the Outdoor Condenser</h2>

<p>Summing the individual room heat loads yields 18,750 BTU of total cooling demand. Rounding up to the next standard outdoor compressor capacity points to a <strong>24,000 BTU (2-ton) multi-zone condenser</strong> capable of powering 3 port connections.</p>
