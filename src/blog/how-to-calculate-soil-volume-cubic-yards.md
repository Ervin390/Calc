<h1>How to Calculate Soil Volume in Cubic Yards & Feet</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 17, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Whether you are leveling a bumpy lawn, backfilling a retaining wall, or planting landscape flower beds, calculating bulk soil volume is essential. Bulk soil suppliers sell topsoil, garden soil, and compost by the cubic yard. Ordering soil without calculating exact volume leads to either extra delivery fees or huge unwanted piles of soil left on your driveway.</p>

<p>Converting garden square footage into cubic yards requires three simple mathematical steps.</p>

<h2>The Soil Volume Formula (Cubic Yards)</h2>

<p>Volume calculation multiplies surface area by depth, then divides by 27 (the number of cubic feet in one cubic yard):</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Cubic Yards = [Surface Area (sq ft) x (Soil Depth in inches / 12)] / 27
</div>

<h2>Practical Soil Calculation Example</h2>

<p>Suppose you are building a large flower garden measuring 20 feet long by 15 feet wide and need to topdress it with 4 inches of fresh topsoil blend:</p>

<ol>
    <li><strong>Calculate Surface Area:</strong> 20 ft x 15 ft = 300 square feet.</li>
    <li><strong>Convert Depth to Feet:</strong> 4 inches / 12 = 0.333 feet.</li>
    <li><strong>Calculate Cubic Feet Volume:</strong> 300 sq ft x 0.333 ft = 100 cubic feet.</li>
    <li><strong>Convert to Cubic Yards:</strong> 100 / 27 = <strong>3.70 Cubic Yards</strong>.</li>
</ol>
