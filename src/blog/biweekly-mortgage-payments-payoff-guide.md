# Biweekly Mortgage Payments: How to Cut Loan Term

Switching from standard monthly mortgage payments to a biweekly payment schedule is one of the most effective strategies to pay off your home loan years early without significantly straining your monthly budget.

By paying half of your monthly mortgage payment every two weeks, you make 26 half-payments a year. This equals **13 full monthly payments per year**-effectively making one extra mortgage payment annually.

## How the Biweekly Math Works

A standard year contains 52 weeks:
- **Monthly Payments:** 12 payments per year.
- **Biweekly Payments:** 26 half-payments per year ($26 \div 2 = 13 \text{ full payments}$).

```
Standard Monthly Schedule: 12 Monthly Payments / Year
Biweekly Schedule:         26 Half-Payments = 13 Full Payments / Year
Extra Principal Paid:      1 Full Payment Direct to Principal Every Year
```

Because that 13th payment is applied directly toward your loan principal, it reduces the overall balance upon which future interest is compounded.

## Worked Example: 30-Year Loan Savings

Consider a **$300,000 mortgage** at a **6.5% fixed interest rate** over 30 years:

- **Standard Monthly Payment:** $1,896.20 / month
- **Biweekly Payment:** $948.10 every two weeks

### Comparison Summary:

| Payment Plan | Total Payments / Yr | Total Interest Paid | Loan Payoff Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Monthly** | 12 | $382,633 | 30 Years (360 Months) |
| **Biweekly Schedule** | 13 equivalent | $298,412 | **24.5 Years** (294 Months) |
| **Total Savings** | **+1 Extra Payment** | **$84,221 Saved** | **5.5 Years Early** |

## 3 Ways to Implement Biweekly Payments

1. **Official Lender Biweekly Program:** Ask your mortgage lender if they offer an automated biweekly drafting program. (Ensure they do not charge third-party setup fees).
2. **Self-Directed Principal Overpayments:** Divide your monthly principal and interest payment by 12, and add that amount to your standard monthly bill marked "Principal Only".
3. **Automated Bank Transfers:** Set up biweekly bill pay from your personal checking account.
