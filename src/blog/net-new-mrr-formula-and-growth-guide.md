<h1>Net New MRR Formula and Growth Guide for SaaS</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Net New MRR measures the net monthly change in recurring subscription revenue. Tracking Net New MRR helps software operators identify whether monthly growth is driven by new logo acquisition or account expansion.</p>

<p>Evaluating Net New MRR prevents team celebrations over top-line subscriber additions when hidden customer churn is silently eroding revenue reserves.</p>

<h2>The Net New MRR Formula</h2>

<p>Net New MRR combines four primary recurring revenue components:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Net New MRR = (New MRR + Expansion MRR) - (Contraction MRR + Churned MRR)
</div>

<p>Let us review an example month:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>New MRR (Newly acquired customers):</strong> +$5,000.00</li>
    <li><strong>Expansion MRR (Tier upgrades & seats):</strong> +$2,500.00</li>
    <li><strong>Contraction MRR (Tier downgrades):</strong> -$1,000.00</li>
    <li><strong>Churned MRR (Cancellations):</strong> -$1,500.00</li>
    <li><strong>Net New MRR:</strong> ($5,000 + $2,500) - ($1,000 + $1,500) = <strong>+$5,000.00 Net Growth</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>Strategies to Accelerate Net New MRR</h2>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Boost Expansion Motion:</strong> Create natural usage triggers that convert active users into higher pricing tiers.</li>
    <li><strong>Implement Pre-Cancellation Offers:</strong> Offer flexible pause options or custom discounts to mitigate churned MRR.</li>
</ul>
