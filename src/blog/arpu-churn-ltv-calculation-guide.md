<h1>ARPU Churn and LTV Calculation Guide for SaaS</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) and monthly account churn rate are the two primary levers governing subscription business unit economics. Together, they dictate customer lifespan, account expansion capacity, and long-term Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).</p>

<p>Understanding how small shifts in ARPU or churn exponentially affect cash retention allows software operators to focus resources on high-leverage growth activities.</p>

<h2>How ARPU and Churn Determine Account Lifespan</h2>

<p>Account lifespan measures the average duration in months a subscriber remains active before cancelling their subscription. Lifespan is inversely proportional to monthly churn:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Average Lifespan (Months) = 1 / Monthly Churn Rate
</div>

<p>Consider the dramatic difference in lifespan across churn tiers:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>5.0% Monthly Churn:</strong> 1 / 0.05 = 20.0 months average customer lifespan.</li>
    <li><strong>2.5% Monthly Churn:</strong> 1 / 0.025 = 40.0 months average customer lifespan.</li>
    <li><strong>1.0% Monthly Churn:</strong> 1 / 0.01 = 100.0 months average customer lifespan.</li>
</ul>

<h2>The Compounding Impact of ARPU Growth</h2>

<p>Increasing ARPU elevates top-line customer value even if churn rates remain constant. For example, if a SaaS company maintains a 2.5% monthly churn rate (40-month lifespan):</p>

<ul>
    <li>At $25 ARPU: 40 months x $25 = <strong>$1,000 Gross LTV</strong></li>
    <li>At $100 ARPU: 40 months x $100 = <strong>$4,000 Gross LTV</strong></li>
    <li>At $250 ARPU: 40 months x $250 = <strong>$10,000 Gross LTV</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>By moving up-market or adding premium features, software teams double or triple LTV without needing to lower account churn further.</p>

<h2>Using the LTV Formula in Strategic Planning</h2>

<p>Combining ARPU, churn, and gross margin yields the net margin LTV formula:</p>

<p><code>Net LTV = (ARPU x Gross Margin %) / Monthly Churn</code></p>
