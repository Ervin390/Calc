<h1>How to Calculate EPDM Roofing Materials Needed</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 6 min read</p>

<p>EPDM (ethylene propylene diene monomer) rubber is the gold standard material for flat and low-slope residential and commercial roofs. Installing EPDM waterproofing requires accurately ordering not just the main rubber membrane rolls, but also bonding adhesive, seam tape, and lap sealant.</p>

<p>Calculating your roofing materials correctly prevents costly job delays or overspending on excess rolls.</p>

<h2>Step 1: Calculate Net Roof Area and Waste Allowance</h2>

<p>Measure your flat roof length and width in feet. Multiply them to find the net square footage, then add a 10% waste factor to account for wall perimeter overhangs and cutting overlap.</p>

<p>For example, consider a 20 ft by 30 ft flat garage roof:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Net roof area: 20 ft x 30 ft = <strong>600 sq ft</strong></li>
    <li>Adding 10% waste allowance: 600 x 1.10 = <strong>660 gross sq ft</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>Step 2: Calculate Membrane Roll Requirements</h2>

<p>EPDM rubber rolls come in standard widths of 10 ft, 15 ft, and 20 ft, usually 50 ft long. Using 15 ft wide rolls on a 600 sq ft roof (15 x 50 = 750 sq ft per roll) requires <strong>1 full roll</strong>.</p>

<h2>Step 3: Estimate Adhesive, Seam Tape, and Sealant</h2>

<p>Complete membrane installation requires accessory supplies:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Bonding Adhesive:</strong> Standard coverage is 60 sq ft per gallon. For 660 gross sq ft: 660 / 60 = <strong>11 gallons</strong> (or two 5-gal pails plus one 1-gal container).</li>
    <li><strong>Seam Tape:</strong> Running one horizontal seam along the 30 ft length requires <strong>30 linear feet</strong> of 3-inch or 6-inch seam tape.</li>
    <li><strong>Lap Sealant:</strong> One tube covers approximately 100 linear feet of exposed seam edge (<strong>1 tube needed</strong>).</li>
</ul>
