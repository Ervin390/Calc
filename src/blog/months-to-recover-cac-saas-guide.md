<h1>Months to Recover CAC Guide for Software Startups</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>For early stage software companies, capital runway is dictated by how quickly sales investments convert back into liquid cash flow. Tracking months to recover CAC provides a clear metric for managing working capital requirements.</p>

<p>When customer acquisition payback takes longer than 18 months in SMB software, cash reserves drain rapidly regardless of top-line MRR growth.</p>

<h2>Why Unadjusted Revenue Distorts Recovery Timelines</h2>

<p>Calculating months to recover CAC by dividing CAC directly by ARPU creates severe inaccuracies. Direct server infrastructure costs, payment processing, and support payroll reduce the dollar amount available to repay sales expenses.</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  True Recovery Months = CAC / (Monthly ARPU x Gross Margin %)
</div>

<p>If CAC is $2,400.00, monthly ARPU is $250.00, and gross margin is 80%:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Unadjusted Revenue Recovery: $2,400 / $250 = 9.6 months (Inaccurate)</li>
    <li>Margin-Adjusted True Recovery: $2,400 / ($250 x 0.80) = <strong>12.0 months</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>Three Ways to Shorten Cash Recovery Times</h2>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Increase Upfront Annual Billing:</strong> Offering a 15% discount for annual upfront commitments eliminates cash recovery lag immediately.</li>
    <li><strong>Raise ARPU with Add-ons:</strong> Introduce expansion seats or premium feature add-ons to boost monthly gross profit per user.</li>
    <li><strong>Optimize Organic Channels:</strong> Build high-intent SEO search traffic to reduce paid ad campaign dependencies.</li>
</ul>
