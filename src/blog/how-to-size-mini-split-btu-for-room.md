<h1>How to Size a Mini Split BTU for Your Room</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Ductless mini-split heat pumps are highly efficient heating and cooling solutions for home additions, master bedrooms, and bonus rooms. However, choosing the wrong unit size creates problems. An undersized unit struggles to cool during summer heatwaves, while an oversized unit short-cycles, failing to dehumidify the air.</p>

<p>Sizing a mini-split correctly requires starting with room square footage and adjusting for ceiling height, sun exposure, and occupancy.</p>

<h2>The Base BTU Sizing Rule</h2>

<p>As a general rule, a standard room requires <strong>25 BTU per square foot</strong> of living space. Multiply room length by width to find square footage, then multiply by 25.</p>

<h2>Worked Example with Room Adjustments</h2>

<p>Consider a 300 sq ft living room (15 ft x 20 ft) with 10 ft ceilings, south-facing sun exposure, and 3 regular occupants:</p>

<ol>
    <li><strong>Base BTU:</strong> 300 sq ft x 25 = 7,500 BTU</li>
    <li><strong>High Ceiling Adjustment (10 ft):</strong> Add 10% (+750 BTU) = 8,250 BTU</li>
    <li><strong>Sunny South Exposure Adjustment:</strong> Add 10% (+825 BTU) = 9,075 BTU</li>
    <li><strong>Occupancy Adjustment (3 people):</strong> Add 600 BTU for the 3rd person = <strong>9,675 BTU</strong></li>
</ol>

<p>Since ductless mini-splits are manufactured in standard size classes (9,000, 12,000, 18,000, 24,000 BTU), round 9,675 BTU up to a standard <strong>12,000 BTU (1-ton) unit</strong>.</p>

<h2>Standard Mini-Split Size Classes</h2>

<ul>
    <li><strong>9,000 BTU (0.75 Ton):</strong> Rooms up to 250 - 350 sq ft</li>
    <li><strong>12,000 BTU (1.0 Ton):</strong> Rooms up to 350 - 500 sq ft</li>
    <li><strong>18,000 BTU (1.5 Tons):</strong> Rooms up to 500 - 750 sq ft</li>
    <li><strong>24,000 BTU (2.0 Tons):</strong> Rooms up to 750 - 1,000 sq ft</li>
</ul>
