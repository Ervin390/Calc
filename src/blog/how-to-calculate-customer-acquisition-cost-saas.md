<h1>How to Calculate Customer Acquisition Cost in SaaS</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 6 min read</p>

<p>Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) measures the total sales and marketing outlay required to acquire a single paying customer. For SaaS startups, tracking CAC ensures marketing budgets drive profitable customer expansion rather than unsustainable burn.</p>

<p>Failing to account for sales team salaries, commissions, software licenses, and agency retainers produces artificially low CAC metrics that misinform financial planning.</p>

<h2>The Blended CAC Formula</h2>

<p>Blended Customer Acquisition Cost encompasses all direct and indirect expenses incurred during an acquisition period divided by total net new customers acquired:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Blended CAC = (Marketing Campaign Spend + Sales Payroll + Software Overhead) / New Customers Acquired
</div>

<p>Let us review a standard monthly scenario for a growing software company:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Paid Ad Campaign Expenses: $10,000.00</li>
    <li>Sales & Marketing Payroll / Commissions: $15,000.00</li>
    <li>CRM & Operational Software Overhead: $5,000.00</li>
    <li>Total Acquisition Expenses: $30,000.00</li>
    <li>New Customers Acquired: 100</li>
    <li>Blended CAC: $30,000 / 100 = <strong>$300.00 per customer</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Expenses to Include in Fully Loaded CAC</h2>

<p>To avoid underestimating your true acquisition cost, ensure your calculation includes:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Paid Advertising Spend:</strong> Google Search, LinkedIn Ads, Facebook Ads, and sponsored newsletter placements.</li>
    <li><strong>Sales & Marketing Headcount:</strong> Base salaries, bonuses, and commission payouts for Account Executives, SDRs, and Growth Managers.</li>
    <li><strong>Tool & Tech Overhead:</strong> Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Apollo, and analytics platform license costs.</li>
    <li><strong>Creative Agencies:</strong> Copywriting, video production, and SEO retainers.</li>
</ul>
