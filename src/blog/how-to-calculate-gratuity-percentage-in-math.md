# How to Calculate Gratuity Percentage in Math

In middle school and high school math curricula, calculating **gratuity** (tipping) is one of the most practical applications of percentages.

Whether solving textbook word problems or calculating dining tips in real life, understanding the formula for percentage gratuity ensures quick and accurate calculations. This guide breaks down the math formulas and mental shortcuts using our [<a href="/tip-calculator/">Tip Calculator</a>](/tip-calculator/).

---

## The Gratuity Math Formula

To calculate the dollar gratuity ($G$) on a bill total ($B$) at a tip percentage rate ($P$):

$$G = B \times \frac{P}{100}$$

$$\text{Total Bill with Gratuity} = B + G = B \times \left(1 + \frac{P}{100}\right)$$

---

## Worked Math Problem Example

**Problem:** A restaurant bill comes to **$65.00**. Calculate a **18% gratuity** and the total bill amount.

### Step 1: Convert percentage to decimal
$$18\% = \frac{18}{100} = 0.18$$

### Step 2: Multiply bill total by decimal rate
$$G = \$65.00 \times 0.18 = \mathbf{\$11.70}$$

### Step 3: Add gratuity to bill total
$$\text{Total} = \$65.00 + \$11.70 = \mathbf{\$76.70}$$

---

## Mental Math Shortcut (10% Method)

To compute gratuity in your head without a calculator:

1. **Find 10%** by shifting the decimal point one place to the left. ($10\% \text{ of } \$65.00 = \$6.50$).
2. **Double 10% to find 20%:** $\$6.50 \times 2 = \$13.00$.
3. **Halve 10% to find 5%:** $\$6.50 \div 2 = \$3.25$.
4. **Combine for 15% ($10\% + 5\%$):** $\$6.50 + \$3.25 = \$9.75$.
