<h2>Understanding 16:9 vs 9:16 Dimensions</h2>

<p>Notice that 16:9 and 9:16 are inverse ratios of each other:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>16:9 Landscape:</strong> The video is wider than it is tall. For every 16 units of horizontal width, there are 9 units of vertical height. This is the global standard for desktop monitors, televisions, and traditional YouTube videos.</li>
    <li><strong>9:16 Vertical:</strong> The video is taller than it is wide. For every 9 units of horizontal width, there are 16 units of vertical height. Designed specifically for smartphone screens held vertically.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Why Instagram Portrait (4:5) Performs Better Than Square</h2>

<p>When posting static images or carousels to your main Instagram feed, using vertical portrait mode (4:5 aspect ratio at 1080x1350 pixels) takes up roughly 20 percent more screen real estate on mobile devices than a traditional 1:1 square photo. Occupying more vertical screen area improves engagement and feed visibility.</p>
