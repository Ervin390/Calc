<h2>Goods & Services vs Friends & Family</h2>

<p>PayPal offers two payment types, but they are intended for entirely separate purposes:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Goods and Services:</strong> Designed for purchasing items, online store orders, and commercial freelancing services. It includes Purchase Protection for buyers and Seller Protection for merchants. PayPal deducts the standard commercial fee from the recipient's payout.</li>
    <li><strong>Friends and Family:</strong> Designed for sending money to friends, splitting dinner bills, or sending gifts. There are no fee deductions for the recipient if funded by bank balance, but it offers zero purchase protection and cannot legally be used for commercial transactions.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Calculate Your Net Deposit</h2>

<p>To calculate how much money you receive after PayPal deducts its fee, apply this formula:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Net Payout = Gross Amount - [(Gross Amount x Percentage Rate) + Fixed Fee]
</div>

<p>For example, on a $500 US commercial invoice:</p>

<ol>
    <li>Percentage fee: $500 x 3.49% = $17.45</li>
    <li>Fixed fee: $0.49</li>
    <li>Total fee: $17.45 + $0.49 = $17.94</li>
    <li>Final Net Deposit: $500 - $17.94 = <strong>$482.06</strong></li>
</ol>
