<h1>How Many Business Days Are in 2026? Year & Quarterly Breakdown</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 17, 2026 · 6 min read</p>

<p>When you are planning payroll, setting quarterly sales targets, or managing corporate project timelines, knowing the exact number of working days in a year matters. A standard calendar year has 365 days, but not all of those days are working days. Weekends and official federal holidays take up a large portion of the calendar.</p>

<p>For 2026, there are <strong>261 total weekdays</strong> (Monday through Friday). Depending on whether your organization observes 10 or 11 standard federal holidays, 2026 contains exactly <strong>250 or 251 business days</strong>.</p>

<h2>Monthly Breakdown of Working Days in 2026</h2>

<p>The number of working days fluctuates from month to month due to weekend placements and February having only 28 days. In 2026, month lengths range from 20 weekdays up to 23 weekdays.</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>January 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (20 Business Days after New Year's & MLK Day)</li>
    <li><strong>February 2026:</strong> 20 Weekdays (19 Business Days after Presidents' Day)</li>
    <li><strong>March 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (22 Business Days)</li>
    <li><strong>April 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (22 Business Days)</li>
    <li><strong>May 2026:</strong> 21 Weekdays (20 Business Days after Memorial Day)</li>
    <li><strong>June 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (21 Business Days after Juneteenth)</li>
    <li><strong>July 2026:</strong> 23 Weekdays (22 Business Days after Independence Day)</li>
    <li><strong>August 2026:</strong> 21 Weekdays (21 Business Days)</li>
    <li><strong>September 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (21 Business Days after Labor Day)</li>
    <li><strong>October 2026:</strong> 22 Weekdays (21 Business Days after Columbus Day)</li>
    <li><strong>November 2026:</strong> 21 Weekdays (19 Business Days after Veterans Day & Thanksgiving)</li>
    <li><strong>December 2026:</strong> 23 Weekdays (22 Business Days after Christmas)</li>
</ul>
