<h1>How to Calculate Profit Margin Percentage: Formulas & Examples</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 17, 2026 · 6 min read</p>

<p>Every business owner needs to understand profit margins. Whether you sell physical products online, run a local retail store, or offer client services, your profit margin determines how much actual money stays in your bank account after covering expenses.</p>

<p>Profit margin measures the percentage of revenue that turns into net profit. If your business earns $100 in sales and incurs $70 in costs, your net profit is $30, giving you a 30 percent profit margin.</p>

<h2>The Profit Margin Formula</h2>

<p>Calculating profit margin percentage requires two basic financial numbers: total sales revenue and total costs (cost of goods sold or operating expenses).</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Profit Margin (%) = [(Total Revenue - Total Cost) / Total Revenue] x 100
</div>

<p>Alternatively, since profit equals revenue minus cost, the simplified formula is:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Profit Margin (%) = (Net Profit / Total Revenue) x 100
</div>

<h2>Step-by-Step Margin Calculation Example</h2>

<p>Let us walk through a practical retail pricing scenario:</p>

<ol>
    <li><strong>Determine your product cost:</strong> You purchase a wholesale item for $40.</li>
    <li><strong>Set your selling price:</strong> You list the item in your store for $100.</li>
    <li><strong>Calculate gross profit:</strong> $100 revenue minus $40 wholesale cost equals $60 gross profit.</li>
    <li><strong>Apply the margin formula:</strong> Divide $60 profit by $100 revenue ($60 / $100 = 0.60).</li>
    <li><strong>Convert to percentage:</strong> Multiply 0.60 by 100 to get a <strong>60% profit margin</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<h2>What Is a Good Profit Margin?</h2>

<p>A good profit margin varies significantly by industry. According to financial benchmark data:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Retail & Grocery:</strong> 5% to 10% net margin due to high inventory turnover.</li>
    <li><strong>E-commerce & Direct-to-Consumer:</strong> 15% to 25% net margin.</li>
    <li><strong>SaaS & Software:</strong> 70% to 85% gross margin, 20% to 40% net margin.</li>
    <li><strong>Consulting & Professional Services:</strong> 20% to 35% net margin.</li>
</ul>
