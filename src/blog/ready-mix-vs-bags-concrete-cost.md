<h1>Ready-Mix vs Bags: Which Concrete Costs Less</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 4 min read</p>

<p>When planning a concrete pour, homeowners must decide between two delivery options: buying pre-mixed concrete from a ready-mix truck or buying 60lb or 80lb paper bags from a local home improvement center and mixing them by hand or with a rented electric mixer.</p>

<p>While bagged concrete appears cheaper per unit, the break-even point in labor and cost occurs surprisingly quickly as volume increases.</p>

<h2>Option 1: Bagged Concrete (DIY)</h2>

<p>A standard 80lb bag of concrete costs around $6.50 and yields 0.60 cubic feet. To pour 1 cubic yard (27 cubic feet), you need 45 bags.</p>

<ul>
    <li>Material cost for 1 cu yd in bags: 45 x $6.50 = <strong>$292.50</strong></li>
    <li>Total weight to carry and mix: 45 x 80 lbs = <strong>3,600 lbs</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>Option 2: Ready-Mix Truck Delivery</h2>

<p>Ready-mix concrete trucks charge between $125.00 and $150.00 per cubic yard for short loads, plus a minimum delivery fee if ordering under 3 to 4 yards.</p>

<p>For a 2 cubic yard patio pour:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Bagged Concrete:</strong> 90 bags x $6.50 = <strong>$585.00</strong> (plus manual mixing of 7,200 lbs of material)</li>
    <li><strong>Ready-Mix Truck:</strong> 2 cu yds @ $135/yd + $100 short load fee = <strong>$370.00</strong></li>
</ul>

<h2>The Financial Break-Even Point</h2>

<p>For small repairs under 0.75 cubic yards (under 35 bags), buying pre-bagged concrete is more economical. For any project exceeding 1 cubic yard, ready-mix truck delivery saves both money and hours of exhausting physical labor.</p>
