# How to Calculate 90 Days From Today

Planning quarterly goals, tracking contract termination windows, or checking visa validity requires calculating 90 days from a specific date. 90 days is roughly three calendar months, but because months alternate between 28, 30, and 31 days, adding three months directly produces errors.

This guide explains how to accurately calculate 90 calendar days and 90 business days from today using simple formulas, calendar rules, and automated tools like our [<a href="/date-calculator/">Date Calculator</a>](/date-calculator/).

---

## Why Adding 3 Months Does Not Equal 90 Days

A common mistake when calculating a 90-day window is simply adding three months to the current date. For instance, moving three months forward from November 14 points to February 14. However, November has 30 days, December has 31, and January has 31.

Counting actual calendar days shows that 30 + 31 + 31 equals 92 days, not 90. If your legal contract specifies a strict 90-day notice period, missing the target date by two days could breach contract terms.

```
Incorrect: Nov 14 + 3 Months = Feb 14 (92 Days)
Correct:   Nov 14 + 90 Days   = Feb 12
```

## Step-by-Step Calendar Method for 90 Days

To manually count 90 days from today without missing days, break the period into month segments:

1. **Count the remaining days in the starting month.** If today is March 10 in a 31-day month, 21 days remain in March.
2. **Add complete middle months.** April has 30 days. Adding April brings the count to 21 + 30 = 51 days. May has 31 days. Adding May brings the count to 51 + 31 = 82 days.
3. **Add the remaining days from the target month.** You need 8 more days to reach 90 (90 - 82 = 8). The target date is June 8.

| Month | Days Added | Running Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| March (remaining) | 21 days | 21 days |
| April (full month) | 30 days | 51 days |
| May (full month) | 31 days | 82 days |
| June (target) | 8 days | **90 days** |

## 90 Calendar Days vs 90 Business Days

Legal agreements and financial transactions often distinguish between calendar days and business days:

- **90 Calendar Days:** Includes every day on the calendar, including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
- **90 Business Days:** Excludes weekends and official holidays. A period of 90 business days spans approximately 18 calendar weeks, or roughly 126 calendar days.

When negotiating contracts or visa stays (such as the Schengen 90/180-day rule), verify whether the requirement specifies calendar days or business days.
