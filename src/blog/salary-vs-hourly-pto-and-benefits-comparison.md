# Salary vs Hourly: PTO and Benefits Comparison

When evaluating job offers or negotiating compensation packages, comparing base pay rates alone can be misleading. A salaried position offering $60,000 per year includes different fringe benefits, paid time off (PTO) rules, and overtime rights than an hourly role paying $28.85 per hour.

This guide compares salaried vs hourly compensation across PTO accrual, health benefits, retirement matching, and FLSA overtime protections using our [<a href="/hourly-to-salary-calculator/">Hourly to Salary Calculator</a>](/hourly-to-salary-calculator/).

---

## Key Benefits Comparison Matrix

| Benefit Category | Salaried Exempt Employees | Hourly Non-Exempt Employees |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pay Predictability** | Fixed check regardless of hours worked | Varies based on actual hours clocked |
| **Overtime Eligibility** | Exempt from overtime pay | Entitled to 1.5x pay after 40 hours/week |
| **Paid Time Off (PTO)** | Standard paid holidays and sick leave | PTO often accrued per hour worked |
| **Health & Retirement** | Full corporate plan access | Full access if working >30 hours/week |

---

## Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual Differences

1. **Salaried Exempt PTO:** Salaried employees usually receive a fixed bank of PTO days per year (e.g., 15 days paid vacation + 10 paid holidays). Taking a 2-hour doctor appointment rarely reduces pay.
2. **Hourly Non-Exempt PTO:** Hourly workers accrue PTO incrementally (e.g., 1 hour of PTO earned for every 30 hours worked). Unworked hours without accrued PTO result in immediate pay deductions.

## Financial Conversion Example

To accurately compare an hourly offer with a salaried offer:

$$\text{Equivalent Salary} = \text{Hourly Wage} \times 2,080 \text{ hours} + \text{Annual Benefits Value}$$

For an hourly wage of **$30.00/hr** ($62,400 base):
- If the hourly position offers no paid holidays (10 unpaid days = 80 hours lost = -$2,400), the effective value drops to **$60,000**.
