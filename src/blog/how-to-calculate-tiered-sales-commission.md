<h1>How to Calculate Tiered Sales Commission Pay</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Tiered commission structures are designed to reward high-performing sales representatives as they surpass sales quotas. Unlike flat commission rates where every deal earns the exact same percentage, tiered plans increase the commission rate as overall sales volume hits specified milestones.</p>

<p>Calculating tiered payouts requires breaking your total revenue into separate brackets and applying the corresponding rate to each portion.</p>

<h2>Worked Example of a 3-Tier Commission Plan</h2>

<p>Consider a sales rep with a quarterly quota plan structured as follows:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Tier 1 (Up to $50,000):</strong> 5% commission</li>
    <li><strong>Tier 2 ($50,001 to $100,000):</strong> 8% commission</li>
    <li><strong>Tier 3 (Above $100,000):</strong> 12% accelerator rate</li>
</ul>

<p>Suppose the account executive closes <strong>$135,000</strong> in total new business during the quarter. Here is the step-by-step calculation:</p>

<ol>
    <li>First $50,000 at 5%: $50,000 x 0.05 = <strong>$2,500.00</strong></li>
    <li>Next $50,000 ($50k to $100k) at 8%: $50,000 x 0.08 = <strong>$4,000.00</strong></li>
    <li>Remaining $35,000 ($100k to $135k) at 12%: $35,000 x 0.12 = <strong>$4,200.00</strong></li>
</ol>

<p>Adding all three tiers yields a total commission payout of <strong>$10,700.00</strong>, reflecting an effective commission rate of 7.93% on total sales revenue.</p>

<h2>Why Companies Use Tiered Accelerators</h2>

<p>Accelerators align sales rep incentives with corporate revenue goals. Higher marginal rates encourage representatives to continue closing deals late in the quarter after achieving 100% of their base quota target.</p>
