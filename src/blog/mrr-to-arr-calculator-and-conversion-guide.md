<h1>MRR to ARR Conversion Guide for B2B SaaS</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 24, 2026 · 5 min read</p>

<p>Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is the core top-line financial metric for evaluating mid-market and enterprise B2B software companies. ARR normalizes recurring subscription revenues into a single annual valuation metric.</p>

<p>While SMB startups focus on MRR, institutional investors and venture capital firms evaluate enterprise SaaS businesses based on ARR run rate multiples.</p>

<h2>The MRR to ARR Conversion Formula</h2>

<p>For standard monthly subscriptions, ARR is calculated by multiplying active MRR by 12:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  ARR = Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) x 12
</div>

<p>For example, if your current monthly recurring revenue is $25,000.00: <code>$25,000 x 12 = $300,000.00 ARR</code>.</p>

<h2>Normalizing Multi-Year Contracts (TCV to ARR)</h2>

<p>Enterprise software contracts often cover 24 to 36 month terms. To calculate ARR from Total Contract Value (TCV):</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  ARR = (Total Contract Value / Contract Duration in Months) x 12
</div>

<p>For a 3-year contract worth $120,000.00 (36 months): <code>($120,000 / 36) x 12 = $40,000.00 ARR</code>.</p>
