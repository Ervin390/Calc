# Time and a Half Pay Rate Lookup Chart

Under the United States Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), non-exempt employees are entitled to **time and a half pay** for any hours worked beyond 40 in a single workweek.

Calculating overtime pay requires multiplying your standard hourly wage rate by **1.5**. This guide provides a direct lookup reference chart for standard hourly rates ranging from $15.00 to $50.00 per hour using our [<a href="/time-and-a-half-calculator/">Time and a Half Calculator</a>](/time-and-a-half-calculator/).

---

## The Overtime Rate Formula

$$\text{Overtime Rate} = \text{Standard Hourly Wage} \times 1.5$$

$$\text{Total Overtime Pay} = \text{Overtime Rate} \times \text{Overtime Hours Worked}$$

## Overtime Calculation Example

An employee earning **$22.00 per hour** works **48 hours** during a single 7-day workweek:

1. **Regular Pay (40 hours x $22.00):** **$880.00**
2. **Overtime Pay (8 hours x $33.00):** **$264.00**
3. **Total Gross Pay:** $\$880.00 + \$264.00 = \mathbf{\$1,144.00}$
