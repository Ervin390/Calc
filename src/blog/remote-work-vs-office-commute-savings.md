<h2>Is a $5,000 In-Office Salary Raise Worth It?</h2>

<p>Suppose an employer offers you a $5,000 annual salary raise to switch from fully remote work to an in-office position requiring 4 days a week on-site. The office is 20 miles away (40 miles round trip).</p>

<ul>
    <li>Annual miles added: 40 miles x 4 days x 50 weeks = 8,000 miles</li>
    <li>Fuel cost (25 MPG @ $3.60/gal): 320 gallons = $1,152.00</li>
    <li>IRS vehicle depreciation ($0.67/mi): 8,000 x $0.67 = $5,360.00</li>
    <li>After-tax value of $5k raise (assuming 25% tax bracket): <strong>$3,750.00</strong></li>
</ul>

<p>In this case, accepting the $5,000 raise results in a net financial loss of $1,610.00 per year after vehicle depreciation and tax deductions are factored in.</p>
