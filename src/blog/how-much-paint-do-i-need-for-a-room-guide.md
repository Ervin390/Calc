<h1>How Much Paint Do I Need for a Room? Complete Guide</h1>
<p class="post-meta">Published August 17, 2026 · 6 min read</p>

<p>Planning a home DIY painting project always starts with one big question. How much paint do you actually need to buy before opening the first can? Buying too little paint leads to mid-project store runs, while buying too much leaves expensive leftover paint cans sitting in your garage for years.</p>

<p>Calculating paint volume is straightforward once you know room surface area and spreading rates. Most standard interior paints cover approximately 350 square feet per gallon on primed walls.</p>

<h2>Step 1: Calculate Total Wall Perimeter & Surface Area</h2>

<p>To find gross wall area, measure the perimeter of your room and multiply it by ceiling height.</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Gross Wall Area = 2 x (Room Length + Room Width) x Ceiling Height
</div>

<p>For example, if your bedroom measures 14 feet long by 12 feet wide with 8-foot ceilings, your calculation looks like this:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Perimeter: 14 + 12 + 14 + 12 = 52 linear feet</li>
    <li>Gross Wall Area: 52 x 8 = 416 square feet</li>
</ul>

<h2>Step 2: Subtract Doors and Windows</h2>

<p>You do not need to paint over glass or wood trim. Professional painters subtract standard allowances for room openings:</p>

<ul>
    <li><strong>Standard Interior Door:</strong> Subtract 21 square feet per door.</li>
    <li><strong>Standard Window:</strong> Subtract 15 square feet per window.</li>
</ul>

<p>Continuing our example room with one door and two windows:</p>

<div style="background: var(--card-bg, #f8fafc); border: 1px solid var(--border, #e2e8f0); padding: 1.25rem; border-radius: 8px; font-family: monospace; font-size: 1.05rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
  Net Area = 416 - (21 x 1) - (15 x 2) = 365 square feet
</div>

<h2>Step 3: Multiply by Number of Coats</h2>

<p>Quality interior painting almost always requires two full coats for rich color depth and durability. Multiply your net area by two:</p>

<p>365 sq ft x 2 coats = 730 square feet of total paint coverage needed.</p>

<h2>Step 4: Divide by Paint Spreading Rate</h2>

<p>Divide your total coverage area by 350 square feet (the standard spreading rate for one gallon of paint):</p>

<p>730 / 350 = 2.08 gallons of paint.</p>
